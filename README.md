# Production ERP & Inventory Manager

A comprehensive, custom-built Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) and Inventory Management system tailored specifically for production environments. This application serves as the operational backbone for tracking the entire lifecycle of materials, products, and ongoing projects within the company.

## 🚀 Business Value & Problem Solved
Managing production inventory with spreadsheets quickly becomes chaotic, leading to inaccurate stock levels, unaccounted leftovers, and project delays. 

This custom ERP solves those issues by providing:
- **Absolute Traceability:** Know exactly what materials are in stock, what was used, and what was left over from completed jobs.
- **Workflow Automation:** Seamlessly transitions materials from purchase orders to stock, and then to specific projects.
- **Cost Efficiency:** By meticulously tracking leftovers and remaining quantities, the company minimizes waste and optimizes purchasing.
- **Centralized Project Hub:** Gives managers a bird's-eye view of all ongoing and completed projects, along with their associated material costs and labor hours.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Comprehensive Inventory Tracking:** Real-time monitoring of product quantities, raw materials, and usable leftovers.
- **Project Lifecycle Management:** Track projects from inception to completion, attributing specific materials, purchase orders, and labor hours to each job.
- **Purchase & Vendor Management:** Streamlined workflows for creating and verifying purchase orders against incoming stock.
- **Labor & Employee Tracking:** Built-in tools for logging employee labor hours directly against specific production projects.
- **User Management & RBAC:** Secure user roles ensuring that production floor staff and administrators only see what they need to.
- **Dynamic Reporting:** Real-time calculations of costs (FIFO), stock forecasting, and project profitability.

## 💻 Tech Stack
This full-stack application was built to be robust, secure, and easily maintainable.

- **Frontend:** React, styled with a modern and intuitive user interface designed for fast data entry on the production floor.
- **Backend:** Node.js / Express API serving highly optimized endpoints.
- **Architecture:** Client-server model with a distinct separation of concerns, ensuring scalable performance even with large datasets.
- **Deployment:** Containerized/Deployable on standard Node.js hosting environments.
